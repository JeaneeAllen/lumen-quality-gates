Method

  • Only rows for the latest balance date are used; the batch's daily snapshots are not summed.
  • Each customer's account balances are summed first; the average is taken over those customer totals.
  • Distinct branches, customers, and accounts present in the validated mart.
  • Accounts are assigned to branches individually, so one customer can hold accounts at several branches. A branch summary counts a customer at every branch where they hold an account and uses only their balances at that branch. Branch balance totals add up to the overall total; branch customer counts can add up to more than the overall customer count.
  • Balances are summed exactly with decimal arithmetic. Averages are rounded to the nearest cent, halves rounded up (ROUND_HALF_UP).
